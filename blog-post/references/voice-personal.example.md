# Personal voice template

Copy this file to `voice-personal.local.md` (gitignored) and fill it in with
your own writing sample and rules. When `voice-personal.local.md` exists, the
skill loads it; otherwise it falls back to this template.

Reference sample: "<title of your piece>" by <you>, <URL or file path>.

Personal voice is an **option**, not a replacement: default stays
explanatory, and the personal voice is applied only when the user asks for it
or points at a personal sample.

## Distilled rules

Replace these with rules distilled from your sample. Examples of the kind of
rule that belongs here:

- Person and tense (e.g. first person, present-tense recollection).
- Paragraph length (e.g. very short, often one sentence).
- Sentence style (e.g. short, few subordinate clauses, concrete nouns).
- Punctuation habits (e.g. em dashes for turns and asides, curly quotes).
- Emphasis style (e.g. a whole line in capitals).
- How a piece ends (e.g. a single-line kicker).
- What to avoid (e.g. headings inside the piece, throat-clearing intros).

## How to mix it with a sourced post

- Personal voice owns the frame (anecdote) and the coda. The explanatory
  middle stays plain so the citations do not fight the prose.
- Citations still attach to every factual claim. A personal voice never
  exempts a statistic from a `[n]`.
- Do not copy the source site's typographic quirks; that is rendering, not
  intent.
- Do not let the kicker replace a sourced conclusion.
